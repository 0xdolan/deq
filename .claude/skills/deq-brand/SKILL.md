# Deq brand

> Phase 0 stub. Phases 2 and 12 fill in the logo construction and brand tokens.

- The logo is a redesigned Deq mark (a chevron with a dot above it), built on the 24-unit grid.
- Kurdish flag: 2:3 ratio, red / white / green bands, a 21-ray yellow sun centred. Verify colors against the Wikimedia Commons SVG before use (candidates: `#ED2024`, `#FFFFFF`, `#278E43`, `#FEBD11`).
- All color pairs must meet WCAG AA where they carry text or meaning.
