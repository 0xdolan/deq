# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in Deq to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Deq is cultural heritage. Treat the communities, artists and people whose stories and images appear here with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and constructive.
- Respect differing viewpoints, dialects and traditions.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Credit others' work and sources.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Hostility based on ethnicity, nationality, language or religion.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainer at <0xdolan@gmail.com>. Reports are handled privately. The full enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
